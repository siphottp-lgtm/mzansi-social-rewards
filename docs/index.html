4-Layer Celo Architecture

1. Community & Mobile Client

Next.js web experience and Capacitor Android wrapper delivering walls, stories, groups, and pages for everyday mobile users.

2. Application & Moderation Layer

NestJS APIs and PostgreSQL data models handling profiles, community posts, eligibility checks, and reward accounting.

3. Celo & KASIC Reward Layer

Low-cost mobile-first transactions on Celo distributing Native KASIC Coin incentives for verified community contributions.

4. Reputation & NFT Tier Layer

On-chain / tiered badges recognizing sustained community builders across a structured 12-week pilot roadmap.

Community Reward Tiers

Bronze Tier

Entry contributor badge for active posting, commenting, and onboarding into local Mzansi groups and stories.

Silver Tier

Consistent community builder tier unlocking higher Native KASIC Coin reward multipliers and group curation perks.

Gold Tier

Top ecosystem steward tier for high-impact local organizers driving sustained engagement and pilot milestones.
